How to interpret

Daily trend is the directional filter. Hourly conditions are the entry confirmation. RSI 70/30 marks overbought/oversold. Z-score uses 20 completed closes. SuperTrend uses ATR(10) with multipliers 1, 1.7 and 3. ATR(14) drives stop references. Initial stop reference is 1.5 × hourly ATR from the latest hourly close; trailing distance is 2 × hourly ATR. Recalculate the stop from your actual fill price before entry.

IV collection

IV Rank/Percentile need at least 20 distinct days of genuine saved constant-maturity IV observations. Missing IV is shown as unavailable, never as zero. IV collection will be addressed separately after the scanner improvements. API documentation: Open API docs.
MarketGreeks is a read-only research tool and does not place orders. Signals use completed candles. A WAIT result or missing/stale data should not be overridden by the dashboard. Check FYERS connection, data freshness, liquidity, spreads and your own risk before any trade.
